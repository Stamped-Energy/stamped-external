Client pack · sample numbers

Ten clear floor actions that need live plant data.

Each card says what to do, why, how, who owns it, which bill line it hits, and the evidence behind it. Four work on most plants. Six are for steel, cement, pharma, or specific machines.

4 agnostic 6 plant / equipment Flip for evidence ← → to navigate

Click a card to flip. Arrow keys or space move between slides.

01 · Agnostic

MD co-start stagger

The MD bill window is 15 minutes or less. You must see feeder overlap live.

02 · Agnostic

ToD peak load shift

Tariff clock + process ready decide today’s move. A fixed night-shift plan is not enough.

03 · Agnostic

Idle auxiliary cut

Idle waste is time × kW. Monthly totals hide the event.

04 · Agnostic

Contract demand soft-landing

CMD risk is decided inside one rolling window. Alarms alone are not a soft-hold list.

05 · Steel · melt shop

Furnace holding cut on delay

Delay flags live in production. Furnace kW lives in EMS. Join them in time.

06 · Cement · pyro + grinding

Kiln + mill stagger / prefer WHR

Watch kiln, mill, WHR, and incomer live together. Do not treat them as four separate screens.

07 · Pharma · batch utilities

HVAC idle-duty drop

Use setback set-points from the approved SOP list only. No free-text changes.

08 · Equipment · compressors

Multi-unit sequencing

You need header pressure plus three power readings. Monthly compressor kWh is not enough.

09 · Equipment · chillers

Approach / condenser waste

Live approach vs outdoor air flags the week to clean. Calendar cleaning is often too late or too early.

10 · Equipment · pumps

Overpressure recirculation trim

High pressure + open bypass + low demand lasts a short time. End-of-shift logs often miss it.

How to use with a client

One agnostic. One that matches their plant.

Walk What → Why → How, then flip for evidence. That is the “only with live data” moment. Stress owner, bill line, and M&V lock. Do not lead with a vague savings claim.

  1. Pick two. One agnostic (MD, ToD, idle, CMD) and one plant or equipment example that maps to their site.
  2. Flip the card. Tags + window + cite show why a monthly bill or single meter cannot issue this in time.
  3. Say the caveat. Figures are samples. A live audit binds tags, tariff, and baseline to their plant.
