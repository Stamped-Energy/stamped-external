01 · Agnostic · MD

Hold the second feeder start 10 minutes

Two big loads in the same MD window — the bill won’t tell you which machines until it’s too late.

02 · Agnostic · ToD / thermal

Gravure dryer warm-up 25 min earlier

Warm-up is eating peak ToD even when output is the same — shift warm-up, not production start.

03 · Equipment · drift

Inspect Compressor 2 filter / unload valve

Compressor 2 is using more power than usual for the same air pressure — nine days. Inspect before extra bill and breakdown.

04 · Agnostic · idle

Switch off packaging line aux when nothing runs 20 min

Conveyors and fans stay on when the line is empty — nobody connects no output to aux power in real time.

05 · Agnostic · ToD

Start batch chiller later — batch still on time

You’re cooling for a batch that doesn’t start for 90 minutes — that’s peak-rate power you can move.

07 · Steel · melt shop

Reduce furnace holding when roll is delayed 45+ min

Holding power keeps running through a delay — melt schedule and furnace kW aren’t looked at together until the shift ends.

08 · Cement · pyro + grinding

Start the mill after the kiln settles

Kiln and mill ramp together every morning — that’s when demand and peak grid import hurt.

09 · Pharma · batch utilities

Trim chillers when the batch hall is empty

Full chiller run on an empty batch block — utilities can trim within your approved temperature band.

10 · Equipment · pumps

Check CW pump P-12 — valve may be stuck recirculating

Pump working harder than needed for the same flow — a two-hour mechanical check, not a vibration project.

How to use with a client

One load example. One that matches their plant.

Read the talk track aloud. Walk What → Why → Owner → Due, then flip for evidence. You already monitor — Stamped helps decide what to do next, checks feasibility, assigns it, and measures the result.

  1. Pick two. One agnostic (MD stagger, ToD warm-up, idle aux, batch chiller) and one vertical or equipment example.
  2. Flip the card. Tags + baseline show why a monthly bill alone cannot issue this in time.
  3. Say the caveat. Figures are samples. A live audit binds tags, tariff, and baseline to their plant.
