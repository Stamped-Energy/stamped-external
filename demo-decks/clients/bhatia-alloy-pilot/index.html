Why this matters

The factory already has the assets. The next advantage is how intelligently it operates them.

Decisions today sit across operators, engineers, planners, ERP, machine data and spreadsheets. The data exists. The intelligence is fragmented. Stamped is one continuous layer across the plant, connecting what is happening on the floor with what should happen next.

What Stamped does

From plant data to decisions that can actually be executed.

01

Connect

Machines, meters, ERP, MES, SCADA and production systems.

02

Understand

Assets, processes, production, energy and operating states, in context.

03

Identify

Deviations, waste, constraints and the opportunities the day hides.

04

Act

A specific prescription, assigned to a person.

05

Verify

Measure what happened after the action, and learn from it.

Every recommendation carries the operating context that produced it. For example:

A generic system says

“Reduce furnace energy consumption.”

Stamped prescribes

Furnace 3 has held operating temperature for 74 minutes with no scheduled batch. If the next batch starts after 16:40, cut the idle hold now and restore temperature 18 minutes before loading.

What we will look for

We don’t want to prescribe the problems before seeing the plant. We want the plant to tell us where the value is.

01

Energy intelligence

  • Idle and standby consumption
  • Specific energy deterioration
  • Peak-demand exposure
  • Heat-treatment efficiency
  • Utility optimisation
  • Solar, grid and load interaction
02

Tooling and assets

  • Tool utilisation and life
  • Tool-related downtime
  • Machine under-utilisation
  • Repeated operating patterns
  • Maintenance and production together
  • Hidden capacity constraints
03

Production and process

  • Dynamic production planning
  • Machine allocation
  • Replanning after a breakdown
  • Process sequencing
  • Bottleneck identification
  • Production and energy trade-offs

Final use cases are selected after the plant survey and data assessment.
