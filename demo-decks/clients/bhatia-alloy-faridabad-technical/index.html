Perceptions 1 and 2 · heat treatment

Do not buy hot idle. Do not start a skinny charge when a compatible lot can fill the basket.

01 · Empty furnace / hot idle

Heat up when the charge is confirmed, not before

Problem
An empty T6 (or powder oven) sits at operating temperature with no metallurgy in progress. You buy wall, storage, and radiation losses for zero kg treated.
Decision
Shift the empty-asset start so temperature arrives with confirmed load readiness, still inside the production window production owns.
Verify
Hot-idle minutes: setpoint reached to charge or load start. Empty and charged tags must agree with the floor.

Lock: never furnace-off or setback with a charge. Never shorten soak. Never delay quench.

02 · Under-loaded HT

Fill the basket or wait for a compatible charge

Problem
A partially loaded cycle spreads fixed furnace losses over fewer kg. Intensity rises even when the recipe is correct.
Decision
Do not start the skinny cycle if a compatible lot exists in the same recipe window and delivery, JIT, and quench stay safe. Production and metallurgy own "compatible."
Verify
Energy per kg versus matched historical batches (same recipe, furnace class, mass band). Quality gates and delivery unchanged.

Lock: no lot mix. No soak cut. No quench delay. If the truck window dies, the card falsifies.

Perceptions 3 and 4 · drift and handoffs

Catch same-output energy drift. Stop ready assets from waiting hot on the next station.

03 · Matched-output drift

Same heat, same output, more energy

Problem
Against a matched baseline (grade, mass band, soak, output), intensity moves up. Something changed in the thermal machine or envelope, not in the production plan.
Decision
Flag drift. Point investigation before the next run: air/fuel, infiltration, seals, dampers, whatever your HT owner already trusts.
Verify
Energy per kg returns toward the matched baseline while temperature profile and quality gates hold.

Lock: no recipe cut as the energy fix. We flag and point; we do not promise "we will find the leak" without meters.

04 · Handoff idle

Ready upstream, next station not ready

Problem
A hot asset stays ready while the next station cannot take work: forge heat waiting on press, empty T6 waiting on charge, rack waiting on hoist. Energy with no additional good parts.
Decision
Assign the handoff so press, charge, hoist, or quench path is ready when the upstream thermal clock clears. Pick one handoff class you recognise.
Verify
Ready-to-take minutes for that handoff class versus your own recent median. Timing first; energy when metered.

Lock: not foundry melt language. Never cut soak or Type III dwell to free the hoist. Live recipes stay do-not-disturb.
