Pillar 1 · Load & Energy Efficiency Intelligence

Manage the whole load, not only the monthly peak

Stamped continuously combines electrical load, production state, operating schedule, and tariff windows to recommend the best practical energy decision available at that moment.

Combine load, schedule, and MGVCL ToD to recommend the best practical move each shift.

Sequence

Stagger large starts, preheat, and utility loading where the schedule has room.

Reduce

Find idle machines, unloaded compressors, excess holding, and off-shift utilities consuming without useful output.

Shift & correct · more levers

Shift

Move flexible demand to better ToD, solar, or captive-generation windows without risking output or quality.

Correct

Act on PF drift, inefficient pressure or temperature bands, contract-demand mismatch, and recurring MD events.

Illustrative application areas: offset makeready · gravure dryer warm-up · compressor and chiller staging · HVAC and extraction schedules · MGVCL ToD windows

Production commitment, quality, and safety remain hard constraints. The lowest-energy option is rejected if it disrupts the job plan.

Pillar 2 · Prescriptive Equipment Intelligence

Each asset is compared with its own normal operating behaviour

Equipment-specific ML models calibrate to Nadiad's operating data after connection. They compare an asset with matched shifts, production conditions, and operating states, so gradual deterioration is not hidden inside a plant-wide average.

Per-asset ML compares each machine to its own baseline — not a plant-wide average.

Baseline model

Learns the expected operating band by time, shift, process state, and production context.

Drift and anomaly model

Flags sustained changes in specific power, duty cycle, startup shape, current/load behaviour, or trip frequency.

Context check

Separates production-driven change from a likely equipment or operating issue.

Prescription and verification

Recommends inspect, clean, or tune; then checks whether the curve recovered after close-out.

Typical signals we watch

Typical signals

  • Compressor specific-power drift at comparable header demand
  • Chiller or cooling efficiency drift under similar ambient/load conditions
  • Dryer fan or exhaust duty changing for matched jobs
  • Repeated starts, trips, or longer ramp behaviour on connected assets

Earlier warning than fixed thresholds. No bearing-failure date or remaining-useful-life claim without the necessary condition-monitoring data.

From decision to floor action

The right action reaches the right owner with its evidence attached

Stamped routes prescriptions by asset and role. Urgent work can require an admin confirmation before release; larger work stays in the queue until an owner and approved window are assigned.

Tap Snooze or Acknowledge on each assigned prescription. Evidence and impact travel with the action.

WhatsApp for assigned actions · dashboard for queue and evidence · export for management review

The floor sees a short action card. Plant leadership sees the queue, constraints, expected value, and realised outcome.

Evidence before credit

Potential becomes realised only after the signal moves

A prescription is not counted as a saving when it is issued or marked done. Stamped locks the pre-action baseline, observes the post-action period, and records realised value only when the agreed evidence clears.

Savings count only after baseline lock, post-action observation, and agreed evidence.

Integration

  • OPC-UA, Modbus, MQTT, API, or scheduled file export, depending on existing access

Boundaries & data governance

Operating boundaries

  • Read-only on OT in the Proof Run
  • No PLC, machine, furnace, chiller, or utility setpoint writes
  • Existing EMS, MES, production, and maintenance systems remain systems of record
  • Human approval for maintenance, schedule, capex, or high-impact actions

Data governance

  • Minimum data scope agreed before connection
  • Role-based access and action audit trail
  • Retention, hosting, and plant-network design agreed with ITC IT/security before deployment
  • Source lineage retained for every prescription

Stamped is not an EMS replacement, MES, CMMS, vibration-monitoring system, solar/EPC service, or autonomous plant-control system.
