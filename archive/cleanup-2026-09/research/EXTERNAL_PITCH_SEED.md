## Canonical spoken / short pitch

Stamped shows the plant operator, in real time, which floor decision is costing money, the one change to make, and who should make it. In one plant, a machine sat ready for 4 hours because its part was stuck in another section, and that wait cost about ₹40,000.

I've spoken with more than 100 operators, plant heads, and maintenance leads, and walked four plant floors.

In every one, operators make efficiency calls without live data from other departments, so one section wins at the expense of the rest.

We're building the one decision an operator can act on, not a plant-wide platform, because the metrics already exist and nobody makes that decision with full data today.

---

## How to use this

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Job** | Name the costly floor decision, the one change, and the owner — in real time |
| **Hero** | One decision an operator can act on |
| **Anti-hero** | Plant-wide platform; more dashboards; metrics without a cross-department decision |
| **Proof shape** | Lived floors and operator conversations; closed decision + owner — not a fixed % savings identity |
| **Example in the first lines** | **Illustrative pitch story only** (arbitrary face-value). Do not promote the ₹40,000 / 4-hour figure as measured product proof, a guaranteed outcome, or a customer case study unless a named plant and evidence path are separately approved |
| **Founder claims** | “100+ operators / four floors” are founder pitch claims — do not inflate; do not invent extra logos or savings |

## Map to product (so pitch and product stay one company)

| Pitch line | Product lock |
|------------|--------------|
| Which decision is costing money / one change / who | Choose → assign → verify; one card; one owner |
| Real time / floor | Next hours / shift horizon — not month-ahead APS |
| Not a plant-wide platform | Wide coverage, narrow authority; five domains on one card; energy is wedge not category |
| Metrics already exist | Read existing systems; do not replace MES/APS/ERP |
| One section wins at expense of rest | Continuity / exception / time domains — cross-department constraint on one card |

Hard stops in `09` still bind in any external claim (no silent plant control, no quality/maintenance takeover, no invented savings).
