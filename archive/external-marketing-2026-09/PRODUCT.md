# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Plant heads, maintenance heads, and electrical heads at large-scale Indian manufacturing sites (e.g. Nestlé / ITC-scale culinary and FMCG plants). They see this work in a short on-site or follow-up walkthrough, often on a phone or tablet passed across the table, not as engineers browsing a platform pack.

## Product Purpose

Impeccable design work in this repository primarily covers **client technical leave-behind decks** (`demo-decks/clients/…`). Success is winning trust in that walkthrough: the buyer believes Stamped can turn plant signals into an assigned next step with owner, effort, rupee impact, and evidence, without replacing their stack or writing to PLCs.

Consistency across decks is secondary to trust for the person in the room. The platform pack (contracts, ADRs) remains engineering SSOT; this file governs **brand/design** surfaces shipped from this repo.

## Brand Personality

practical · rigorous · unflashy

Voice: plain plant language, named owners, evidence before credit. Confident without sales gloss. Prefer one concrete prescription over abstract platform claims. De-emphasize AI/ML theater; lead with load, asset health, assignment, and verification.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS purple / glow dashboards
- ESG brochure gloss
- Agentic AI hype slideware
- Dense consulting PowerPoint walls of text
- Fake live plant KPIs or undefendable savings guarantees
- Mid-word wrapping, clipped prescription fields, or mobile chrome covering content

## Design Principles

1. **Win trust in the room:** the walkthrough must feel honest and usable before any data ask.
2. **Show the next action:** owner, effort, due window, and evidence; not another dashboard.
3. **Plant language over product jargon:** no undefendable line or brand claims inside prescriptions.
4. **Honesty first:** illustrative is not measured; read-only OT; write "rupees," never overclaim savings %.
5. **Mobile is first-class:** full prescription fields readable; nothing clipped under nav.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Touch targets ≥44px on phone and tablet. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Copy and UI must stay readable under meeting-room lighting on a phone held at arm’s length.
