# What we show in decks

Agent summary of **Proof Runs** (industry) and **11-scene client briefs**. Full HTML lives under [`../../demo-decks/`](../../demo-decks/). Public site voice: [`../website/`](../website/). Client narrative order: [`../client/POSITIONING_AND_NARRATIVE.md`](../client/POSITIONING_AND_NARRATIVE.md).

## Product claims (always)

| Say | Do not say |
|-----|------------|
| Read-only overlay on meters / SCADA / BAS / bills | EMS / MES / CMMS / PLC write / plant OS replacement |
| **Industry Energy Management** + **Asset Health Intelligence** | Invent a third SKU or “agentic AI” as the lead |
| Rupee-scored / ₹-scored prescriptions with owner + due | Guaranteed savings without locked M&V |
| Verified with evidence (telemetry; bill can confirm later) | Vibration PdM, RUL %, “fine-tuned before connect” |

## Two deck families

| Family | Shape | When |
|--------|-------|------|
| **Industry Proof Run** | Longer walkthrough: title → hook → savings map → what → Rx → floor → verify → tech → audit → boundaries → video → live → offer | Cement / steel / pharma discovery |
| **Client technical brief** | **11 scenes:** title → gap → fit → load → equipment → prescription → agentic → floor → verify → integration → offer | Named or anonymous leave-behinds |

Named-account quality bar: Nestlé Pantnagar (uses the word **rupee(s)** on amounts). Anonymous forge-HT must not name LNM.

## Client-facing pillars (deck language)

1. **Industry Energy Management** — MD, idle, HVAC, tariff / ToD; monthly rupee impact.  
2. **Asset Health Intelligence** — early equipment issues with economic ranking; same evidence trail.

Do **not** lead with agentic AI. Agentic layer = how a finding becomes a feasible assigned action at scale.

## Banned / dropped phrases (current leave-behinds)

Do not restore these in new deck copy unless product explicitly revives them:

- Pay-as-you-save  
- ₹1–5L as a product promise band  
- “Signals become work orders”  
- Em dashes as a style tic  
- IIT as the close / credibility closer on the offer slide
