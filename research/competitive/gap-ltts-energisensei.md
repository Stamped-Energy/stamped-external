# LTTS EnergiSensEI vs Stamped — plain English

## In one sentence
L&T Technology Services’ **EnergiSensEI** is a global industrial energy & sustainability platform that **checks meter data against engineering physics before AI recommends**, then helps plants act — sold with LTTS consulting, build, and managed operate services.

## What they actually do (no jargon)
- Pipeline: **Verify → Learn → Recommend → Act**. Act means they support the decision; **the plant team makes the call** (human-in-the-loop emphasis on the product page).
- Five named agents: **Optimizer** (live waste flags), **Trends** (demand forecast / early problems), **Insights** (plain-language Q&A), **Reports** (audit-ready D/W/M), **Dashboard** (exec and shop floor).
- Use cases: compressed air leaks, HVAC/thermal set-point advice, motors/rotating faults, boilers & steam loss, multi-site demand/loss flags, ESG & compliance.
- Journey: **Assess → Build → Operate → Sustain** with a global managed-services team — platform + ER&D services under one roof.
- Meter-agnostic; works with SAP, Power BI, SCADA/IoT; edge or cloud. Not a meter OEM.
- Loud on **ISO 50001** one-click cycles and emissions/ESG reporting (claims large cuts in reporting effort).
- Public pages are **thin** on India-specific DISCOM Time-of-Day / Maximum Demand packaging and on PAT / BRSR Core.
- Marketing savings and site counts vary across the page (treat as claims; numbers are inconsistent).

## What Stamped already does in the same area
- We already do human-in-the-loop Findings → Prescriptions → ack/done/verify — and we refuse silent PLC writes.
- We already flag compressed air / compressor waste and have HVAC / equipment-health rules in catalog.
- We already offer Ask Analyst (read-only Q&A) and Forge dashboards.
- We already score India HT money levers (ToD, MD, PF, idle) in ₹ from bills/tariffs — an area where their public materials look weaker.
- We already use a defendable baseline path (TOW-P as money-of-record; fancy forecast models stay in shadow) — philosophically close to “don’t trust bad data.”
- We specify ISO/BRSR as evidence enable, not as a services-led EnMS operate contract.

## What they can do that we cannot (the real gaps)

### Physics-verify-before-AI as a marketed product step
- **What it is:** Every meter/sensor reading is checked against engineering physics before AI learns or recommends; bad/drift/offline data is caught early.
- **Why a plant would care:** Plants have been burned by dashboards that believed a drifted meter; CFOs want defendable numbers.
- **Software, hardware, or both?** Software.
- **Can Stamped add this?** Yes software — strengthen residual/physics gates and Lab dual-lane; keep black-box forecast out of money-of-record.
- **Should we?** Yes — name and productize the honesty gate we already philosophically hold.

### Five-agent suite packaging
- **What it is:** Buyer-friendly “Optimizer / Trends / Insights / Reports / Dashboard” story over one pipeline.
- **Why a plant would care:** Easier to buy “an AI platform” than a pile of engines.
- **Software, hardware, or both?** Software (UX/packaging).
- **Can Stamped add this?** Yes software over existing L3–L6 — not a new pillar.
- **Should we?** Maybe later as packaging; Priority is still Prescription cards, not agent theatre.

### Demand forecast / Trends as an operational product
- **What it is:** Forecast demand and surface problems before they hit the P&L.
- **Why a plant would care:** Early warning for energy and production planning.
- **Software, hardware, or both?** Software.
- **Can Stamped add this?** Yes as advisory/Lab; No as autonomous forecast-as-₹-of-record.
- **Should we?** Advisory later; never let forecast invent bill savings.

### HVAC “tunes set points” vs human Act
- **What it is:** Use-case language that sounds like the software adjusts HVAC set points; Act stage still says humans decide.
- **Why a plant would care:** Comfort/energy tradeoffs without waiting for a monthly review.
- **Software, hardware, or both?** Software (± OT write).
- **Can Stamped add this?** Human-guided soft setpoint writeback = **specified, not shipped** (Wave C). Silent autonomous tune = No.
- **Should we?** Ship Wave C carefully where deals need “Act”; never silent write.

### Assess → Build → Operate → Sustain managed services
- **What it is:** LTTS sells metering assessment, OT integration, and ongoing operate — not just licenses.
- **Why a plant would care:** One throat to choke for multi-year EnMS programmes; global SI wallet.
- **Software, hardware, or both?** Services.
- **Can Stamped add this?** Yes with partners — Stamped stays product.
- **Should we?** Partner SI delivery; do not become an ER&D body shop.

### Water + multi-utility + broad GHG Scope 1–3 / SBTi suite
- **What it is:** Energy, water, emissions, ESG in one sustainability desk (sibling utility-monitoring materials reinforce this).
- **Why a plant would care:** Corporate sustainability teams want one vendor for everything.
- **Software, hardware, or both?** Software.
- **Can Stamped add this?** Yes for Scope 2 evidence next to energy; No for full WAGES/ESG platform (out of two pillars).
- **Should we?** Scope 2 evidence only; park the rest.

## Bottom line for Stamped
EnergiSensEI is an **enterprise SI / packaging competitor** — moderate threat when the PO is “LTTS will Assess–Operate our global EnMS,” low threat on pure India DISCOM ToD/MD Prescription deals. Their recommend-then-human-call loop is close to ours; their edge is physics-verify storytelling, agent packaging, ISO one-click, and services attach. Copy the verify-first narrative and evidence reports; partner for build-operate; do not chase WAGES or forecast-as-money. Ignore 5–15% / site-count marketing in our proof.
