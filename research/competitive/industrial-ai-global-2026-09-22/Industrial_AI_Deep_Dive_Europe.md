## Dossiers — Europe priority

### 1. Siemens Industrial AI / Industrial Copilot / Senseye
- **HQ / website:** Munich; https://www.siemens.com/global/en/products/automation/topic-areas/industrial-ai.html
- **What they sell:** Industrial Edge, digital twins (Xcelerator), Industrial Copilot (engineering/ops/maintenance GenAI), Senseye predictive maintenance (acquired 2022), shopfloor agentic initiatives with partners.
- **Buyer:** Global discrete + process manufacturers already on Siemens automation.
- **Tech approach:** Copilots on engineering tools + APM; Senseye ML on maintenance data; twins for simulation; edge inference near machines.
- **Public finances:** Siemens AG group revenue via Siemens IR (Industrial automation is a segment — use latest annual report; do not invent AI-only revenue).
- **Press example:** Siemens expands Industrial Copilot with generative AI maintenance offering (Senseye-linked). https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-expands-industrial-copilot-new-generative-ai-powered-maintenance-offering
- **Stamped adjacency:** **Foundation** both products. Partner/compete carefully — never position as PLC/DCS replacement.
- **Risks:** Procurement gravity; long cycles; advisory copilots ≠ verified Energy M&V.

### 2. ABB Ability / Genix Copilot
- **Website:** https://new.abb.com/process-automation/genix/abb-genix-copilot · https://www.abb.com/
- **What they sell:** ABB Ability industrial portfolio; Genix Industrial IoT & AI Suite; Genix Copilot (GenAI on APM — alert prioritization, RCA, NL interaction, work-request drafting).
- **Buyer:** Process industries (metals, mining, pulp, chemicals, energy).
- **Tech approach:** OT/IT/ET data into Genix → analytics + GenAI copilots; cloud partnerships (Azure OpenAI narratives historically).
- **Revenue:** ABB group IR; Genix-only Not disclosed publicly.
- **Stamped adjacency:** Process Adjacent; Energy Adjacent in process utilities.
- **Risks:** Installed-base lock-in; Stamped should ride beside, not against.

### 3. Schneider Electric + AVEVA + Cognite
- **Websites:** https://www.se.com/ · https://www.aveva.com/ · https://www.cognite.com/
- **What they sell:** Energy management + industrial automation + AVEVA industrial software; Cognite Data Fusion agreed to be acquired for **$3.1B** (Jun 30, 2026) and integrated with AVEVA post-close. Cognite 2025 revenue >$170M; ~800 people (**Schneider/Reuters**).
- **Sources:** https://www.reuters.com/business/schneider-electric-buy-ai-software-firm-cognite-31-billion-2026-06-30/ · https://www.cognite.com/en/company/newsroom/schneider-electric-announces-agreement-to-acquire-cognite
- **Also:** Schneider–Microsoft Industrial Copilot narratives (Automate 2025 PR).
- **Stamped adjacency:** Energy **Foundation** (Schneider energy DNA) + Process Foundation (AVEVA/Cognite data plane).
- **Risks:** Mega-stack consolidation may raise switching costs; also creates clearer “app layer” opportunity for outcome specialists.
- **Deal status note:** Agreement announced; closing subject to regulatory approvals — treat as **to-acquire**, not closed, until completion PR.

### 4. Bosch Rexroth / ctrlX
- **Website:** https://www.boschrexroth.com/ (ctrlX AUTOMATION)
- **What they sell:** Automation platform (ctrlX) with app ecosystem, Linux-based control, connectivity for machine builders.
- **Stamped adjacency:** Foundation for discrete machine OEMs.
- **Funding/revenue:** Bosch group; ctrlX-only Not disclosed publicly.

### 5. Celonis
- **HQ / website:** Munich; https://www.celonis.com/
- **What they sell:** Process mining / process intelligence — discover how work actually flows in ERP/IT systems; execution apps. Manufacturing use cases exist (order-to-cash, production process mining) but Celonis is **not** OT process-control AI.
- **Funding:** ~€2.2B raised across rounds; valuation historically $13B+ in press — **press**. https://funding.tech.eu/companies/37B6813B-B1EA-4A07-879E-93393A1B79C1
- **Stamped adjacency:** Energy Adjacent (utility process mining rare); Process **Adjacent** only for IT-side process, **not** methods engineering. Avoid conflating.
- **Risks:** Buzzword collision on “process.”

### 6. Software AG / Cumulocity IoT
- **Website:** https://www.softwareag.com/
- **What they sell:** Cumulocity IoT platform for device/asset connectivity and industrial IoT applications; enterprise integration heritage.
- **Ownership note:** Software AG taken private / ownership changes in recent years — confirm current branding before sales conversations.
- **Stamped adjacency:** Foundation.
- **Revenue:** Historical Software AG IR; current private figures Not disclosed publicly.

### 7. Oden Technologies
- **HQ / website:** Stockholm / New York; https://oden.io/
- **What they sell:** AI products for manufacturing process optimization — collect factory data, surface drivers of quality/throughput, recommend actions (AI suite launch with Series B).
- **Funding:** Series B **$28.5M** led by Nordstjernan Growth (Apr 2024); total funding ~$58.7M reported in coverage. https://siliconangle.com/2024/04/11/oden-technologies-raises-28-5m-launch-ai-driven-products-manufacturing/
- **Buyer / verticals:** Discrete and process manufacturers seeking production optimization.
- **Stamped adjacency:** Process **Direct** peer (optimization outcomes). Energy Adjacent if energy is a modeled driver.
- **Risks:** Crowded “AI for manufacturing” messaging; need KPI specificity.

## Enrichment — Europe majors’ agentic stack (what “copilot” means in practice)

### Siemens Industrial Copilot (practical)
- **Engineering:** Suggest PLC/HMI code, explain errors, accelerate TIA Portal-class work.
- **Operations:** Natural language queries over plant context (when connected).
- **Maintenance:** Senseye-linked predictive + generative troubleshooting; Siemens press claims pilots with **~25%** less reactive-maintenance time — **vendor-claimed**. https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-expands-industrial-copilot-new-generative-ai-powered-maintenance-offering
- **Stamped angle:** Copilot reduces engineer toil; it does **not** own verified ₹/SEC or cycle-time outcomes. Complementary.

### ABB Genix Copilot (practical)
- Sits on Genix APM: prioritize alerts, draft RCA narratives, create work requests via NL. https://new.abb.com/process-automation/genix/abb-genix-copilot
- Stamped angle: Same — advisory layer on APM, not Energy M&V product.

### Schneider × Microsoft × (future) Cognite
- Energy management DNA + AVEVA industrial software + Cognite contextualization (post-close) = strongest **Energy+data** mega-stack globally.
- Stamped Energy in large Schneider accounts should plan for **integration**, not displacement.

### Braincube vs Oden (EU outcome specialists)
- Braincube: broader IIoT platform + optimization apps; large growth equity (€83M).
- Oden: sharper “AI products for manufacturing” packaging post-$28.5M Series B.
- Both closer to Sight Machine / process optimization lane than to Stamped Process methods-engineering lock — still useful Process study objects for production-linked KPIs.

### PhysicsX vs Neural Concept
- PhysicsX: industrial engineering / process physics AI at large capital scale ($300M Series C press).
- Neural Concept: design/simulation acceleration for R&D — wrong champion for plant PE freezes unless DfM loop is explicit.
