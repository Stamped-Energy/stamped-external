## Method

- WebSearch + WebFetch of primary sources (company About/Product/Press, SEC filings, Reuters/TechCrunch/company PR, Chinese IR/press where needed).
- **No invented statistics.** Unknown figures marked `Not disclosed publicly`.
- Vendor marketing marked **vendor-claimed**.
- Contradictions (funding databases, site metric inconsistencies) flagged in-place.
