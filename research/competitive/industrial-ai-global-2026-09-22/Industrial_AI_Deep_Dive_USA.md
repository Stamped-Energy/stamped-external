## Dossiers — Priority USA / NA companies

### 1. Bright Machines
- **Legal / HQ / founded / website:** Bright Machines, Inc.; San Francisco, USA; founded ~2018; https://www.brightmachines.com/
- **What they sell:** Software-defined manufacturing platform (**Bright Factory**): virtual product development (Bright Designer), AI robotics (Smart Skills), and Factory Intelligence (Bright Data) for electronics / AI hardware assembly.
- **Buyer / champion / verticals:** Electronics OEMs, ODMs, contract manufacturers; AI infrastructure hardware (servers, racks, storage). Champion: manufacturing / automation leadership.
- **Tech approach (plain English):** Capture design intent → automate assembly with vision+ML robotics → feed production data back into design and programming. Partners: Microsoft Azure, NVIDIA Omniverse narratives.
- **Funding:** Series C $126M (Jun 2024): $106M equity led by BlackRock funds/accounts + NVIDIA, Microsoft, Eclipse, Jabil, Shinhan; $20M venture debt J.P. Morgan. Total raised **>$400M** (**vendor PR**). https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bright-machines-raises-126m-series-c-funding-to-propel-manufacturing-into-software-defined-era-302181151.html
- **Revenue / ARR / plants / employees:** Not disclosed publicly.
- **Named clients:** Specific end-customer names largely not disclosed in Series C PR; customer categories chipmakers/OEMs/ODMs/CMs (**vendor**).
- **Pricing:** Not disclosed publicly ( CapEx+software engagement model typical of SDM).
- **Stamped adjacency:** Process **Adjacent** (execution/automation data loop). Energy **Irrelevant**.
- **Risks:** Capital-intensive robotics; wrong CapEx model for Stamped wedge. Study data closed loop only.
