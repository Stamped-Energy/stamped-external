## Detailed India dossiers

### Greenovative — Direct Energy
- **What they sell:** Prescriptive energy intelligence for multi-site industrials: source mix (grid/solar/DG), renewables ROI gaps, capex utilization vs OEM rating, production unit economics (energy↔output), enterprise benchmarking.
- **Go-live (vendor FAQ):** 2–3 weeks on existing SCADA/PLC/BMS/IoT — no retrofit required (**vendor**).
- **Buyer:** Energy/electrical + digital/I4.0 heads; enterprise multi-plant groups.
- **Public testimonials (site):** Automotive conglomerate digital head; Jotun MEIA; cement IT; LG Ranjangaon; textiles E&I — **vendor site**.
- **Claims:** 80+ industry leaders; deployments across 7 countries; 12–15% energy cost cut; <12 mo ROI — **vendor-claimed** (https://greenovative.com/).
- **Funding:** European sustainability strategic investment (amount often undisclosed). https://greenovative.com/news-events/greenovative-secures-european-strategic-investment/
- **Pricing:** Not disclosed publicly.
- **Stamped battle card:** Match time-to-first-prescription; beat on **board-defendable M&V** and process-state-aware energy (regimes), not on dashboard beauty.
- **Risks:** Already in enterprise accounts Stamped wants; retention claim high — switching cost.

### ZeroWatt — Direct Energy
- **What they sell:** “Digital energy brain” — detect waste, root-cause, prescribe; equipment health angle; commercial model in press: subscription per metering point, hardware at cost.
- **Press scale:** YourStory Jun 2025 — NTPC-veteran founders; **100+ factories**; **120 MW+** connected load; ~10% avg energy reduction; ~78,800 tCO₂/yr avoided — **press/vendor**. https://yourstory.com/2025/06/zerowatt-energy-ai-efficiency-platform
- **Site claims:** May state 20–30% annual energy cost reduction / 3–6 month payback — **vendor**; reconcile with press carefully.
- **Stamped battle card:** Decide HW strategy explicitly (ZeroWatt is HW-assisted). Differentiate on verification + multi-site enterprise (Greenovative’s strength) + Process join.
- **Risks:** Hardware ops burden; % claim variance across sources.

### LTTS EnergiSensEI — Direct Energy (SI/trust)
- **What they sell:** Energy & sustainability platform; **verify physics before AI**; agent suite; Assess→Build→Operate→Sustain.
- **Claims:** 5–15% energy & water reduction; payback figures vary; site counts inconsistent (50+ vs 1000+) — **flag**. https://www.ltts.com/solutions/EnergiSensEI
- **Buyer:** Enterprises wanting SI accountability + ESG reporting.
- **Stamped battle card:** **Copy the doctrine** (meter truth before models). Productize M&V packs faster than an SI engagement.
- **Risks:** Competing with LTTS services machine on large accounts; partner possibility.

### Infinite Uptime — Adjacent (PdM scale teacher)
- **TechCrunch Mar 10, 2025:** Series C **$35M** (Avataar, StepStone, LGVP; Tiger Global, GSR existing); ~**$65M** total; **~800 plants / ~30 countries**; ~350 employees; 2× revenue growth claim 3 years; operationally cash-flow positive (**founder via press**). https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/10/infinite-uptime-bags-35m-to-help-factories-optimize-equipment-usage/
- **Tech:** Piezoelectric sensors for harsh/high-temp/acidic environments; patents claimed (~5); AI diagnostics; OEM partnerships.
- **Verticals:** Steel, cement, metals, mining, fertilizers, chemicals, paper.
- **Energy angle:** Founder explicitly investing in less energy per ton from existing capacity (**press**).
- **Stamped:** Do not build PdM. Learn multi-country heavy-industry GTM and energy-per-ton messaging adjacency.

### Nanoprecise — Adjacent (ECM)
- Series C **$38M** equity+debt (Mar 2025). https://nanoprecise.io/blog/nanoprecise-closes-38m-usd-series-c-fundraise/
- Energy-Centered Maintenance: maintenance decisions framed through energy waste of unhealthy assets.
- Stamped: Steal framing for Energy×reliability co-benefit narratives; still not a PdM product line.

### Lambda Function — Process Direct-adjacent

## India Energy competitive triangle (monologue)

“Three shapes own India Energy talk-track today. **Greenovative** is HW-agnostic enterprise prescriptive SaaS with multi-site benchmarking. **ZeroWatt** is expert-as-software with metering-point economics and hardware at cost. **LTTS EnergiSensEI** is engineering-verified SI-grade trust. Stamped must pick a clear shape: I recommend **prescriptive + verify-first** — Greenovative’s actioning speed with EnergiSensEI’s M&V spine — and join Process regimes so energy prescriptions know when the plant changed grade or tooling.”

## India Process competitive reality (monologue)

“India Process AI is **not** yet a Fero/Imubit density market. Closest peers are **Lambda Function** (machining autonomy) and global CAM AI (CloudNC) entering shops. MES/costing tools (Dashnode/Emithran/Hulu-like) crowd the ‘digital manufacturing’ phrase. Stamped Process wins by refusing that crowd: manufacturing engineering decisions, freeze discipline, implementation path including tooling.”
