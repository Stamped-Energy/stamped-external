# Competitive set — who owns the next operating action

**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Status:** Research for the coarse architecture. Not company identity.  
**Authority:** If this file conflicts with [`09-stamped-founder-vision.md`](09-stamped-founder-vision.md), `09` wins.  
**Method:** Public sites and press, September 2026. A performance number on a vendor page is a **vendor claim**, not a measured fact.

Stamped helps plant teams choose, assign, and verify the next operating action across energy, cost, time / throughput, continuity / flow, and short-horizon exceptions. One card, one owner, honest close. This note names who publicly sits on that job, and who sits beside it.

## Direct — the next operating move

These vendors publicly aim at a bounded next move under live conditions.

### Sight Machine

Agent-powered industrial software that maps a plant into a semantic model and runs agents that investigate production. The public job is investigation, then a live recommendation to operations, with an enterprise agent surface and optional simulation before a trial. Deploy-in-days and output-gain figures on the homepage are **vendor claims**. Delivery described in public pages is recommendations into tools operators already use. Agent crews are described as gaining permission over time. That is the closest public comparison, and it is an investigation layer, not one card with one owner and a verified close.

Sources: [sightmachine.com](https://www.sightmachine.com/), [agentic platform note](https://www.sightmachine.com/news-agentic-manufacturing-platform), [enterprise agents](https://www.sightmachine.com/platform-enterprise-agents).

### Fero Labs

Explainable process models that recommend adjustments, with an optional live mode that can send a bounded offset to existing control after engineers set limits. The default described in public FAQ material is a human applying the recommendation. The job is a setpoint or adjustment, not a cross-domain card with a single owner and a closure that includes rejection and no measurable change.

Sources: [ferolabs.com](https://www.ferolabs.com/), [live optimization FAQ](https://www.ferolabs.com/insights/post/faq-what-is-live-optimization).

### Braincube

Real-time process steering. Public material describes prescribed settings aligned to operators and, in some accounts, fed toward control. Deploy-in-days language is a **vendor claim**. The unit of work is closer to a setting than to an owned decision with an honest non-success.

Sources: [braincube.com/platform](https://braincube.com/platform/), [AVEVA Braincube page](https://www.aveva.com/en/products/braincube-the-leading-industrial-analytics-for-process-health/).

### TwinThread

Industrial cloud with digital twins and packaged modules (centerline, energy, production) that recommend or operationalize setpoints. Public copy says people can override. The product story is stabilize-then-optimize modules, not one queue across five domains.

Source: [twinthread.com](https://www.twinthread.com/).

### C3 AI Process Optimization

Dynamic setpoint recommendations on an agentic platform, with operator-guided flows. A multi-month deploy figure on the product page is a **vendor claim**. The job is process setpoints, not a short-horizon exception card.

Source: [c3.ai process optimization](https://c3.ai/products/c3-ai-process-optimization/).

### Zentio

An agentic model of machines, people, and materials for high-mix plants. The public action is reschedule or dispatch under disruption, with human approval before master-data writes. That overlaps “what do we do in the next few hours” for discrete plants, and it is a planning surface. Stamped does not publish a new schedule.

Source: [zentio.ai](https://zentio.ai/).

## Adjacent — platform, analytics, or system-of-record agents

### Seeq

Agentic investigation over time-series and documents, with optional closed-loop hooks through extensibility. The primary surface is analysis and reports.

Sources: [seeq.com](https://www.seeq.com/), [Seeq Intelligence announcement](https://www.seeq.com/resources/press-releases/introducing-seeq-intelligence/).

### Cognite

An industrial knowledge graph and an agent workbench. Customers build agents. Write-back is whatever those agents are wired to. There is no standard one-card closure product in the public agent docs.

Sources: [Cognite Atlas](https://www.cognite.com/en/product/atlas), [Atlas concepts](https://docs.cognite.com/cdf/atlas_ai/concepts).

### Pelico

Agents for production, supply, and maintenance recovery against ERP data. The job is supply-and-execution orchestration.

Source: [pelico.ai/agentic](https://www.pelico.ai/agentic).

### QAD Champion AI

Role agents on ERP, supply, and production transactions. Governed automation of back-office and planning exceptions. A 90–150 day outcome figure is a **vendor claim**.

Source: [qad.com/champion-ai](https://www.qad.com/champion-ai).

## Reviewed and left beside this set

Palantir Foundry positions ontology-wide actions and a manufacturing operating system. MachineMetrics is moving toward discrete execution. Guidewheel is visibility and tier meetings. Augury and Instrumental are machine health and inspection. HighByte is a connectivity and namespace layer. Tulip is frontline apps. None of those is the same job as one owned operating card.

## Architectural bets

These are product-structure choices, not slogans.

1. The unit of work is one decision card with open, owned, and closed states. Investigation threads are not the product.
2. Five domains share one card. A primary domain names the decision. Secondary domains are separate sections. They are not five queues and not one summed savings line.
3. Learning uses verified, no-change, and rejected closures. A recommendation that was merely sent is not a lesson that the action worked.
4. One owner per card. The role is chosen from a configured set. The person is resolved at assignment.
5. The default is that a person acts. An autonomous class stays off until Stamped certifies it and a named plant owner enables it.
6. The first closed card does not require a plant-wide semantic model. Context is admitted for a named decision family.

## Sight Machine, specifically

Sight Machine is the clearest public comparison for “agents that understand the plant.” Stamped does not win by copying the semantic-model program, the scheduling agent, or a path toward autonomous operations. Stamped wins by recording one next action, one owner, and an honest close.
