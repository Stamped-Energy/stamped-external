## Cross-cutting themes already in prior research

1. **Closure loop** (state → Rx → human → evidence) is the repeated north star.  
2. **Hours / continuity / dual wallet** already exist beside kWh — under-marketed as “plant efficiency.”  
3. **Production as hero pillar** is researched and **parked** (ADR/MES brief) — exploration ≠ reopen without founder ask.  
4. **Competitors** split: energy prescribe (₹), OEE dashboards, process AI (setpoint/yield), PdM hardware.  
5. **Docs/MES/QMS** unlock methods, scrap, schedule — named in research but weak in current L1.
