## 1. Cross-cut findings

1. **Category is manufacturing efficiency, not energy.** Vaibhav + ground pilots + Vision v0.3 company line agree. Energy is a high-ROI *lever* and a clean proof path, not the whole cost stack (quality, downtime, cycle time often dominate).

2. **Global market is crowded by archetype, not by one competitor.** USA/EU: foundation (Cognite/Palantir/majors), outcome specialists (Fero, Imubit, Braincube, Oden, Jemba, MadeOS), PdM hardware, vision, CAM, physical AI. India: Greenovative/ZeroWatt own energy prescribe; Infinite Uptime/Nanoprecise own PdM scale.

3. **Software-only “data → ranked action → human” is a real lane.** Closest rhymes: Jemba, MadeOS (API path), Green Factory advisory, Braincube guidance, Fero explainability, Guidewheel meter-derived ops — not BrightAI stickers or Bright Machines cells.

4. **Current L1 intake funds modes 1–8 in `01` strongly; Process freezes (mode 11) do not.** Vision v0.3 Process is directionally coherent but needs drawings/tooling/docs intake before it is honest as a shipped product.

5. **Service vs product is the existential risk.** Faridabad CNC cycle-time wins (**founder-reported**) read as service unless productized for multi-site / repeated engagement kernels (Vaibhav).

6. **Candidate sequencing hypothesis (not locked):** Option **D** in `05` — efficiency wedge on software connectors now → Process beachhead later — best balances software-only, India pilots, and Vaibhav scale advice.
