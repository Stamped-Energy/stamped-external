## 2. BrightAI (bright.ai) — absorb vs ignore

**What they sell:** Physical AI / “Stateful” OS for essential infrastructure and manufacturing: continuous sight (stickers, drones, wearables, edge models) → predictive intelligence → **guided field execution**. Manufacturing examples include acoustic early friction (line-stop prevention) and measurement-to-production accuracy (e.g. Latham Pool case — **vendor**).

| Absorb | Ignore for near-term Stamped |
|--------|------------------------------|
| Multi-KPI efficiency (throughput, failure prevention, workforce productivity) | Stateful Sticker / drone / wearable hardware SKUs |
| Observe → prioritize → assign human action | “Foundation models on trillions of proprietary sensor readings” as a claim we copy |
| Frontline guidance UX pattern | Competing as a sensor company |

**Software-only analog:** existing meters, SCADA, CNC/MTConnect, bills, calendars = our “sight”; prescriptions + WhatsApp/dashboard = guided execution.

## 5. India monologue for Stamped (exploration)

India Energy peers validate **pay for prescribe + ₹**. Vaibhav + ground reality push the category to **plant efficiency**. Differentiation vs Greenovative/ZeroWatt is not “more AI” — it is (a) broader outcome families (time/utilization/methods) when data allows, (b) evidence discipline, (c) path to a **scalable** product rather than plant-by-plant service. Physical AI vendors define the language of continuous observation and guided work; Stamped should speak that language on **customer-owned** OT/IT first.
