# Near-term — L3 detection ceiling

**Status:** Direction open. Encouraged.  
**Authority:** [`../../technical/STAMPED_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../../technical/STAMPED_ARCHITECTURE.md) §6a · ADR-015

Today’s L3 core (scheduler paths, dual-lane emit, Finding contract, no DB URL, calculator-owned money) is the **floor**, not the ceiling.

**Do**

- Invest in better detectors, methods, features, evals, and multi-signal condition finding.
- Keep condition keys, decision-family ids, evidence-tiered facts, verification plans.

**Do not**

- Freeze today’s energy-engine catalog as the ambition.
- Let Lab promote.
- Invent rupees in L3.

Fine design of the uplift belongs in the L3 repos.
